A Cozy Morning

Readiness Level: 0

It's morning. You emerge from a dream, wrapped warmly in your bed. A warm splash of sunlight shines through a crack in your bedroom curtains, and the early morning sounds of spring filter in through the window. You look at your phone and realize it's Saturday and you have no responsibilities today. Should you: get up or go back to sleep?
