I hope you enjoy my final project!

I thought about calling my game "overwhelm" because it reminds me of trying to fight your way through crowds or stressful days. The game starts out innocently enough, but it becomes harder and harder to collect the lights as more and more obstacles buffet the player around the play screen. I eventually added a powerup functionality to decrease obstacles at higher levels because the game got so frustrating by the end. This also added meaning to the score, allowing for a lower "high score" at the end if you used too many powerups to decrease the difficulty.

Thanks for a great semester!

GAMEPLAY:
1. Controls: WASD

2. Collect yellow "lights" to increase score and complete the level!

3. Red obstacles will slightly bounce the player, change the background color, and increase with levels, making light collection more difficult and increasing player frustration.

4. Green powerups decrease the difficultly level by eliminating some obstacles, but also decrease your total score. Try to get to level 10 without using powerups to get the best score!
